## 1. Question-bank and paper-assembly sites (组卷网 / 学科网, 菁优网): tagging, difficulty, assembling papers by knowledge point

### Takeaway
Both sites are teacher tools whose practice-relevant mechanic is the "specification table" (细目表) paper builder: the teacher picks knowledge points, chapters or textbook-original questions, and the system fills a paper; the same engine is now offered to students to assemble papers from their own mistakes. A Chinese patent from a different vendor (蓝鸽, not shown to be what 组卷网 or 菁优网 use) documents a concrete assembly algorithm: a greedy, coverage-first selection filtered by difficulty and discrimination, with a required mastery level per knowledge point. Neither store page documents a difficulty rating, so difficulty grading remains a gap.

### Cited Findings
- 菁优网 (Jyeoo) app listing [V]: claims over 30 million questions and over 2 million papers with 10,000+ added daily, nine subjects. Teachers choose questions by knowledge point (知识点挑题), chapter (章节挑题) or paper (试卷挑题). v5.1.8 added a "textbook original question" filter (课本原题) to chapter and knowledge-point selection; v5.1.4 added topic-based selection (专题挑题) and a "top 30 knowledge points" option. — [App Store 菁优网](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id862280922)
- 菁优网 assembly modes [V]: AI assembly (AI组卷), photo-based assembly, knowledge-point/chapter selection and a specification-table mode (细目表组卷); download as Word or PDF with editable formulas; v5.0.9 added a multi-question basket and batch score entry. — [App Store 菁优网](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id862280922)
- 菁优网 student side [V]: the listing describes the loop "photo search - practice - collect - analyze - mistake training" (拍搜-练习-收录-分析-错题训练); students can batch-delete mistakes, export them as images, and edit the knowledge point on image-only mistakes; v5.1.4 added topic-based paper assembly from mistakes; v5.1.1 added "similar paper" practice in homework reports; v5.2.4 (Oct 27, 2025) added a student learning report; v5.2.6 (Nov 28, 2025) added AI tools for students and parents including paper erasure, AI grading and similar-paper generation; v5.3.6 ("Oct 9", year not shown) added oral-calculation grading in photo search. The page does not describe a difficulty rating or filter. — [App Store 菁优网](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id862280922)
- 组卷 (XKW / 学科网) app listing [V]: developer 学科网（北京）股份有限公司; claims over 10 million questions and over 1 million papers, 10,000+ updated daily; a "complete tag system" (完整的标签体系) with knowledge-point explanations; assembly by AI (AI组卷), smart assembly (智能组卷), chapter, paper, photo (拍照组卷) and specification table (细目表组卷); teachers can photo-upload answer sheets for learning-status analysis (学情分析); students get mistake summaries and mistake training (错题训练). Update notes mention an upgraded similar-question recommendation algorithm. The page does not mention difficulty levels. — [App Store 组卷](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/%E7%BB%84%E5%8D%B7-%E4%B8%AD%E5%B0%8F%E5%AD%A6%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E9%A2%98%E5%BA%93/id1589882227)
- Patent CN109783601A, "computer intelligent paper-assembly method based on test knowledge points" (applicant 浙江蓝鸽科技有限公司; filed 2018-11-23, granted as CN109783601B on 2023-03-28) [V]: inputs are the test scenario, scope, target paper difficulty and discrimination; a knowledge-point set where each point has a required mastery level (four levels, from "apply" down to "no requirement"); a paper template (question types, count per type, points per question); and a bank in which each item is tagged with type, knowledge points, difficulty and discrimination. Algorithm is a deterministic greedy loop, not a genetic algorithm: take the uncovered point with the highest required mastery and the hardest remaining question type; query items matching type, point, target difficulty and discrimination; exclude items covering already-examined points; prefer the item covering the most points in the domain, break ties by higher discrimination then randomly; mark its points as examined. Worked example: a 10-question English unit test with difficulty window 0.4-0.6 and discrimination above 0.3 produced difficulty 0.5, discrimination 0.3 and 14 knowledge points with no repetition. No time-limit input. — [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN109783601A/zh)

### Inferences
- The two site apps confirm that knowledge-point tags are the primary index in the Chinese ecosystem (select by point, assemble by point, analyze by point, and collect mistakes by point); difficulty is a secondary filter, at least in the public descriptions.
- The patent gives a directly implementable "test blueprint" (specification table) for Toishi's practice tests: per skill, a required level (recall, understand, apply), per paper, item-type counts and a difficulty window, and a rule that no skill is examined twice unless the blueprint says so. Because Toishi generates variants, it can set difficulty by generator parameters instead of mining a bank.
- "Textbook original question" (课本原题) as a filter shows that teachers want a canonical-versus-variant distinction; this fits the 母题 (parent problem) idea in the first notes.

### Gaps
- Neither listing explains how difficulty or knowledge-point tags are produced (human editors, model, or response statistics); no help-center page was reached. Zujuan and Jyeoo web pages (zujuan.xkw.com, jyeoo.com) were not fetched; only App Store text was.
- Question counts differ between vendors (30M vs 10M) and are marketing scale numbers; no independent audit.

## 2. School analytics platforms (智学网, 好分数, 七天网络, 极课大数据): score and knowledge-point diagnostics, and how weak points flow back to practice

### Takeaway
These platforms start from teacher marking of school exams (scan or online marking) and give students and parents score, lost-points and knowledge-point analyses; the loop back to student practice is described only in generic terms ("personalized learning path", "tiered homework"). I could not retrieve the 智学网 "personalized learning handbook" (个性化学习手册) mechanics from any primary source, so it remains unverified.

### Cited Findings
- 好分数 parent edition (Haofenshu, developer listed as Guangzhou Aolunzhi Information Technology Co., Ltd.) [V]: built on school exams and diagnostic analysis; parents can view learning-status analysis, subject analysis, lost-points analysis (失分分析) and paper analysis (试卷分析); exam big data and AI "precisely diagnose knowledge gaps" and give "a personalized academic improvement plan"; a college-admissions module (升学宝) is included. 错题 and specific improvement suggestions are not named on the page. Latest version 3.28.33 (10 June). Student and teacher editions are in the first notes. — [App Store 好分数家长版](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/%E5%A5%BD%E5%88%86%E6%95%B0%E5%AE%B6%E9%95%BF%E7%89%88/id1112859166)
- 七天网络 (Septnet, 安徽七天网络科技有限公司) teacher app [V]: mobile marking, online or in-class quiz assignments, exam data analysis; uses image recognition and deep learning; dynamic data collection and big-data analysis "to build adaptive study materials and learning paths for students"; shows "precise analysis of each student's performance on individual sub-questions". The page does not mention a mistake book or knowledge points (the only mistake-related note is wrong-word statistics in an English dictation module). — [App Store 七天网络](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/1239085125)
- 极课大数据 (Jike Big Data), 36Kr feature dated 2017-04-13 [V, company-supplied figures]: starts with electronic marking of homework and exams (saves teachers about 40-60 minutes a day); question-bank data comes 73% from weekly practice tests, 18% from exams and 9% from homework, with the claim that organized tests reduce non-intellectual noise (such as laziness) in difficulty and error-prone-point estimates; each student has a learning file (exams, homework, weak knowledge points) that teachers use to assign homework in different tiers (分层作业) and difficulty levels; schools can use a cloud bank or build their own, with Jike managing categorization; adaptive teaching is described as a later stage, not a current feature; 1,800+ active schools, about 32 million student homework and exam records. No independent outcome data and no mistake-notebook feature mentioned. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/1721475334145)
- 智学网 (Zhixue): the App Store listing and rating were covered in the first notes; this session's attempts to read the student-edition listing and to find any page describing the personalized learning handbook (个性化学习手册) returned only app-store results and no description of the handbook. — [App Store 智学网](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id981030648) (from first notes)

### Inferences
- The consistent school-platform pattern is: per-sub-question data (not just totals) is the raw material; analysis is by knowledge point; the teacher then tiers homework. For Toishi, the transferable part is accepting per-question outcomes from outside the app and mapping them to skills ("mock import"), and showing lost points by skill.
- 极课's design note (practice tests give cleaner difficulty and error-prone estimates than homework) suggests weighting timed, closed-book practice-test results more heavily than casual practice when estimating skill difficulty and weakness.

### Gaps
- No usable description of 智学网's personalized handbook, its knowledge-point report format, or its mistake push; 好分数 student-edition wrong-question features are not documented in the sources reached.
- 极课's article is from 2017; no current (2025-2026) product description was reached, and 七天网络's page gives no mastery model.

## 3. Learning devices (学习机) and the 2025-2026 AI tutors: iFlytek, 学而思, 作业帮, 猿辅导, 有道, 小度

### Takeaway
All device makers advertise the same practice loop (test, learn, practice; locate weak knowledge points; push variants; AI tutor that hints instead of telling), and in 2025-2026 added step-level handwriting grading and prerequisite tracing. The most concrete, usable mechanics: 学而思's per-step handwriting grading with hints (not answers), its prioritization of "not mastered, error-prone and must-test" questions, iFlytek's error-cause classification and cross-grade prerequisite tracing, and Youdao's IRT-based question pushing with gem-style mastery display (a 2023 hands-on review). All effect numbers are vendor-reported with no methodology. 小度 was not researched.

### Cited Findings
**学而思 (Xueersi)**
- Learning machines launched 2025-05-07 (P, S, T series) [V, relayed by iFengTech from 智东西]: 精准学 (precision learning) upgraded to version 3.0, linking learning-status data with the knowledge graph. Its "filter-learn" (过滤学) auto-generates a course mind map; "filter-practice" (过滤练) analyzes in-device practice and teacher-assigned homework, finds "not-yet-mastered questions, error-prone questions and must-test questions" (未掌握题、易错题和必考题) and prioritizes the key ones to reduce blind drilling. The "小思AI 1对1" tutor guides proactively, points out errors, analyzes causes and, in the demo, gives hints on problems in the solution process instead of the standard answer; error diagnosis uses multimodal grading backed by a large-model-driven "step-level knowledge graph" (步骤级知识图谱), with no further technical detail. The dual-core model pairs 九章 (MathGPT) with DeepSeek. The article does not mention a mistake notebook. — [iFengTech](https://tech.ifeng.com/c/8jAfs3a4ATK)
- T4 + 小思AI 1对1, QbitAI, 2025-11-11 [V]: the student writes each solution step on scratch paper; the tutor recognizes and grades each step in real time, points out wrong steps with the reason and correct approach, copes with messy handwriting or paper that already has writing, changes explanation angle if the student keeps erring and adjusts pacing; builds a dynamic learner profile and personalized plan from behavior data. The only external validation cited is that 九章 passed CAICT (China Academy of Information and Communications Technology) first-round education-model evaluations (education model level 5, education agent level 4+); no accuracy or learning-outcome figures. — [QbitAI](https://www.qbitai.com/2025/11/350840.html)

**科大讯飞 (iFlytek)**
- AI学习机 T90 series (T90 Pro, T90 Lite), reported as announced at a 2026-02-28 spring launch [V]: a "test-learn-practice" (测-学-练) loop identifies weak points before building a personalized path; the T90 Pro error-cause feature "locates the cause of the error and gives heuristic hints" through the AI tutor 晓悦 instead of simply explaining the problem; junior-high problems can be traced back to primary-school gaps; middle-school content is tailored to local exam emphasis (examples: Beijing, Jiangsu, Anhui); English listening and speaking mock tests replicate the exam procedures of 20 provinces; vendor-reported "internal test" results: time spent filling gaps on the same chapter down 64%, mastery up 3.1 times; across 50,000+ schools in 32 provinces, low-efficiency study down 55% and motivation up 29%, with no methodology. — [Sina](https://news.sina.cn/sx/2026-02-28/detail-inhpkhwi9956243.d.html). Date note: the first notes cite a 2026-07-02 article for "T90, 18 upgrades"; this Sina piece dates the T90 series launch to 2026-02-28, so the July item may be a later upgrade announcement. Not resolved.
- A promotional SMZDM post on an iFlytek machine (flagged on fetch as containing AI-generated content, so low reliability) [V, low reliability]: capture happens at grading (camera, OCR, Spark LLM for step scoring of subjective answers; known OCR errors from lighting and handwriting, with a manual-correct entry); an "AI traceability engine" (AI溯源引擎) classes errors such as calculation error vs concept confusion; for a wrong question the system "automatically generates several variant questions on the same knowledge point" and uses the knowledge graph to find "prerequisite gaps across grades"; the post credits IRT (item response theory) and the knowledge graph; claims a bank of 5.6 million real exam paper sets. — [SMZDM](https://post.smzdm.com/p/ad7456px/)
- Earlier coverage in the first notes: knowledge network claimed at 1.12 billion points; AI错题本 on T30 Ultra/Pro. — [Sohu](https://m.sohu.com/a/1033840717_134152/) (from first notes)

**有道 (Youdao)** (2023 hands-on, so an earlier hardware generation than the 2025-2026 products above)
- Parent's two-month review of the AI学习机 X10, posted 2023-01-18 [A]: the 错题本 auto-collects wrong answers from practice, labels the error cause, prompts correction, and splits mistakes into "to be corrected" (待订正) and "corrected" (已订正); the AI precision-learning module captures the learning trail and updates a knowledge graph in real time, showing each knowledge point's mastery with "gem" icons; the learning report (学情报告) puts each unit in one of four bands (excellent, good, medium, not yet up to standard: 优秀/良好/中等/待达标); question pushing uses "IRT dynamic question pushing" (IRT动态推题) with difficulty in up to 5 levels; stage tests (unit, midterm, final) run as timed papers; the paper library holds provincial real exams. The reviewer's claimed score gains are personal experience only. — [SMZDM](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a5oxrl83/)
- 子曰 / 2024 education model coverage and current Youdao devices: search returned only headlines (ifanr, QbitAI, chinanews); pages were not read.

**作业帮 (Zuoyebang) / 猿辅导 (Yuanfudao)**
- Covered under consumer apps (section 4). Market context in the first notes (shares, bank sizes).

**Independent view**
- Rest of World (2024) on iFlytek, Baidu, BBK, Gankao tablets [A]: a parent said the essay-scanning feature worked about half the time and that content showed factual errors with weak quality control; a former Squirrel AI employee said "these tablets don't offer much beyond resources already available online"; a Gankao contractor said most video classes have under 20% open rate and under 10% completion; the article gives no systematic evidence of academic gains. — [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2024/china-student-tablet-ai/)
- An ITHome headline (seen only in search results, page not fetched) describes the 2026 AI learning-machine market as moving "from feature stacking to effect verification" (从"功能堆砌"到"效果验证"). — [ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/976/090.htm) [R, headline only]

### Inferences
- Step-level handwriting grading (学而思) is the newest mechanic and the one hardest to copy offline; the transferable part is the interaction: grade the first wrong step, give a hint rather than the answer, escalate only if the hint fails.
- "Must-test, error-prone, not-mastered" (必考/易错/未掌握) is a three-factor priority score that maps to exam weight, observed lapses and low retrievability; this is a cheap addition to an FSRS queue for exam-goal mode.
- iFlytek's cross-grade prerequisite tracing, if it works as described, is the skill-graph walk-down Toishi can do natively; the evidence for it working is vendor-only.
- Youdao's four-band display and gem-style node icons are low-cost UX that make a skill graph legible at a glance.

### Gaps
- 小度, 有道 current devices, 作业帮 learning-machine mechanics and 猿辅导 小猿学练机 practice mechanics: not retrieved beyond the first notes (ITHome pages on 作业帮 devices failed to load).
- No independent test of any claimed result (64% less gap-filling time, 3.1x mastery, 55% less low-efficiency study).
- 讯飞星火 (Spark) practice behavior beyond the above: not retrieved.

## 4. Consumer practice and mistake-notebook apps (猿题库, 小猿AI/小猿口算, 作业帮, 错题Plus, 错题打印机, 错题星, 伴鱼, 金榜举一反三, 豆包爱学)

### Takeaway
The mistake-notebook (错题本) apps converge on one loop: capture (photo, grading or in-app miss) then tag knowledge point (and sometimes cause) then push same-knowledge-point variants (举一反三, "do one question, master the whole class": 做一题会一类) then re-grade then update. The distinctive implementations are 错题Plus (auto-pushed review paper graded online, then re-pushed), 错题打印机 (blank-paper restoration and mistake-to-paper assembly with PDF printing), 伴鱼 (review reminders on the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve) and 豆包爱学 (free, grouped by knowledge point, runs on Apple-silicon Macs). None publishes outcome data. Of the tools reached, only 伴鱼 and Japan's Monoxer describe forgetting-curve-based scheduling; no FSRS use was found.

### Cited Findings
- 错题Plus (developer 上海启本信息科技有限公司) [V]: photo/scan capture with automatic handwriting removal; organized by subject, with category and knowledge-point tags that feed a weak-point analysis; the app auto-pushes a "growth-plan mistake review paper" (成长计划错题复习卷), the student submits it for online grading, which "updates mistake data and intelligently pushes" the next round; 举一反三 added in v1.7.9 (2021); micro-lessons for the related knowledge point (about 12,000) and same-type practice; custom papers from a bank of 25 million+ questions, export to Word; one-tap printing. No standalone analytics dashboard described. — [App Store 错题Plus](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id1476015217)
- 错题打印机 (developer 杭州科熠网络科技有限公司) [V]: scan test papers, erase handwriting to restore a clean copy (v2.0.0, 2023-04-19); box-select questions, store by subject with notes, and assemble a practice paper from mistakes (错题组卷); one-tap HD PDF and printer connection; personalized review plan based on mistakes; AI tutor added 2024-05-30; v2.3.0 (2025-03-31) streamlined mistake-to-paper assembly and added one-tap photo search with AI tutoring; no redo mode or analytics dashboard described; subscription pricing ¥18/week to ¥168 lifetime. — [App Store 错题打印机](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E9%94%99%E9%A2%98%E6%89%93%E5%8D%B0%E6%9C%BA-%E8%87%AA%E5%8A%A8%E6%B8%85%E9%99%A4%E6%89%8B%E5%86%99%E7%AC%94%E8%BF%B9-%E8%AF%95%E5%8D%B7%E9%94%99%E9%A2%98%E6%95%B4%E7%90%86/id1598835335)

### Inferences
- Two ideas recur that are not in the first notes: (a) the mistake record has an explicit state (待订正 to-be-corrected / 已订正 corrected) and the system builds the next test from it automatically; (b) printed or PDF output is a first-class feature (错题打印机, 错题Plus) because students retest on paper; for an offline Mac app a PDF export of a fresh-variant retest paper (with answer key page) is the equivalent.

### Gaps

## 5. Practice-book series structures (五年高考三年模拟, 天利38套, 必刷题, 金考卷, 一本涂书, 高考真题分类汇编) and Japanese/Chinese comparables

### Takeaway
I could not reach a usable structural description of 5·3, 天利38套, 金考卷, 一本涂书 or 高考真题分类汇编 (Baike was blocked for the first researcher and was not retried; Douban and retail searches returned unrelated 2000s books or product listings; one article site disallowed fetching). - 青チャート (Aoi Chart), 《改訂版 チャート式 基礎からの 数学Ⅰ＋Ａ》 by 数研出版, published 2026-01-26 [V]: worked examples (例題) composed of "problems that build basic skill" and "problems suited to consolidating application", spanning textbook to hard-university level; the main value is said to lie in the 指針 (guideline), which explains where a problem's key points lie and how to devise the solution; EXERCISES and a comprehensive practice section (総合演習) at the back; solution videos for the examples; aims at understanding solution strategy rather than memorizing. - From the first notes: 必刷题 (basic and advanced sections per chapter, topic modules, mock sets, 真题) and 赤本 (one full untimed pass, then timed per major question, repeat to three circles, then a shortened-time rerun). — [Sina](https://sina.cn/news/detail/5277040501592120.html); [Kyogakusha guide](https://akahon.net/doc/howto_2012.pdf) (from first notes)

### Inferences
- Architecture common to all structured series: (1) a tiered index of points by importance or frequency, (2) topic modules, (3) timed mixed sets of increasing realism, (4) recombined real items, (5) separate analysis. Toishi's exam goal can mirror this as a ladder of practice-test types.
- Frequency tiers (high/medium/low/basic) are a cheap exam-weight signal for the priority score.
- Answer analysis separated from questions, plus a "technique handbook", matches the Toishi split between solving and the "What went wrong?" diagnosis.

- 5·3, 天利38套, 金考卷, 一本涂书, 高考真题分类汇编: not reached; do not state their structures without a publisher or retailer description. Taobao/JD/Dangdang product pages did not load usable text (JD returned a category listing; a zizzs.com article was robots-disallowed).

## 9. Evidence that these mechanics work

### Takeaway
There is almost no independent evidence for any named tool. The best randomized-style data are two sets of short studies of Squirrel AI (one company-authored paper; one peer-reviewed study with outside analysts per the first notes, funding not verified), which show moderate effects over 2-3 days against teacher-led classes. Everything else is vendor-reported, predictive-accuracy-only, or satisfaction data. General retrieval-practice evidence (first notes) is stronger than any tool-specific evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Squirrel AI (松鼠AI), company-authored conference-style paper (arXiv 1901.10268) [E, conflict of interest]: Study 1 randomized (stratified blocks) 203 Chengdu students aged 13-15 (101 adaptive, 102 teacher-led; 90 and 73 completed) over a 3-day schedule: gains 9.38 vs 1.81, Hedges' g = 0.68, ANCOVA F(1,160) = 16.80, p < .001; Study 2 randomized 140 Zhengzhou students (70 each; 104 completed) comparing it with BOXFiSH plus teacher classes over 2 days: gains 5.86 vs 1.04, g = 0.49. Limitations flagged on review: 2-3 days only, 26% attrition in Study 2, authors employed by the company, some reported ratios inconsistent with the reported means, the Study 2 control group had teacher support, test reliability unreported. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.10268)
- Squirrel AI, Wang, Christensen, Cui, Tong, Yarnall, Shear & Feng (2020), Interactive Learning Environments (abstract via SRI) [E]: two efficacy studies with random assignment of eighth-graders in two provinces, finding larger mathematics gains for Squirrel AI than for whole-class or small-group instruction by expert teachers; the SRI page gives no sample sizes, durations or effect sizes. — [SRI](https://sri.com/publication/education-learning-pubs/when-adaptive-learning-is-effective-learning-comparison-of-an-adaptive-learning-system-to-teacher-led-instruction)
- Ma, Chen, Liu & Yan (2024), Frontiers in Psychology [E, small]: 120 rural Hubei primary students (grades 4-6) randomized; analysis only on the 33 with persistent errors (16 vs 17); a schema-based memory method ("number as image, add-fact as short story", 20 min/day for 15 days) cut persistent errors (post-test mean 0.31 vs 1.0; d about 1.6), where a "persistent error" is the same wrong answer given two or more times for an item. This is error correction by replacing a wrong retrieved answer, not error analysis; control students got no attention-matched activity; no follow-up. — [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1276914/full)
- KAIST behavioral-training record (n = 45, about 14% improvement in the trained group) [E, weak; see section 6]. — [KAIST DSpace](https://dspace.kaist.ac.kr/handle/10203/208802)
- Embibe ESQ paper: predictive accuracy only (see section 8). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02629v2)
- Vendor-only or satisfaction-only: iFlytek T90 (64% less gap-filling time, 3.1x mastery; Sina), Classting (91.5% prediction accuracy; Venturesquare), Monoxer (81.6% satisfaction; ASCII), Embibe blog claims (10% and 23% gains, 2015-2016), 极课 (2017 scale numbers). — see sections above.
- Carried over from first notes [E]: retrieval practice beats restudy on delayed tests (Hui et al. 2021; Zhou et al. 2017 review). — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s10648-021-09604-x); [ECNU](https://jps.ecnu.edu.cn/EN/Y2017/V40/I4/913)
- Failure evidence [R]: Korea's AI digital textbooks lost half their adoption in one semester and their textbook status within five months of launch. — [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2025/south-korea-ai-textbook/)

### Inferences
- For design purposes, treat the Chinese/Korean/Japanese/Indian tool mechanics as "convergent practice" (many vendors independently built the same loop) rather than "proven"; build in the app's own measurement (retention at delay by cause tag and by tier; predicted versus actual practice-test score).
- The only mechanism with a specific small controlled test is persistent-error replacement; the idea is cheap to implement (detect repeated identical wrong answers) and its effect size, while large, is from a very small, young sample.

### Gaps
- No independent RCT of any 学习机, 错题本 app, 智学网 or 好分数 was found; no CNKI search was possible. No evidence was found about Korea's or Japan's apps beyond satisfaction.

## 10. The 15 mechanics most worth adopting, ranked

### Takeaway
Ranked by (a) how many independent tools converge on the mechanic, (b) fit with a free offline FSRS practice tool that serves fresh variants, (c) implementation cost, (d) evidence. Items 1-9 are the core; 10-15 are valuable second-wave features. Context used for "fit": local Mac app, English-only, open-source problems, one daily FSRS queue plus flashcards, skill graph, placement test, step-level "What went wrong?", mistakes bank, exam goals, practice tests, Anki-style card import, practice not teaching. (Context from the project brief and the user's project notes.)

### Cited Findings
(See sections 1-9 for sources; the entries below are my synthesis and reference those sources.)

### Inferences

**1. Mistake lifecycle with explicit states and an auto-built redo packet.** Source: 错题Plus (auto-pushed review paper, graded, then re-pushed), 有道 X10 (待订正/已订正), 伴鱼 (collect, diagnose, 举一反三; forgetting-curve reminders), 小猿AI and 菁优网 loops [V/A]. Fit: each miss creates a mistake entry in a state machine: open, corrected (user viewed and confirmed the fix), re-tested (a fresh variant of the same skill passed), retired (three clean blind timed solves; from the first notes' 赤本 rule). Each miss also queues a redo packet of fresh variants due same day, about a week and about a month on top of FSRS; a second failure flags "needs reinforcement". Cost: low-medium; the data already exists.

**2. Attempt-quality labels from timing: Perfect / Wasted / Overtime / Lucky.** Source: Aakash CBT and Embibe (wasted, overtime, perfect; first-look accuracy; "wasted attempts have to go to zero") [V], PW's time-pressure tag [V]. Fit: label every attempt from correctness, response time against the skill's personal target time and an optional one-tap confidence ("unsure"); "Lucky" is correct but unsure. Feed "Overtime" into the FSRS grade as Hard, "Wasted" into a carelessness counter, and show the mix on the post-test screen. Cost: low; no content needed.

**3. Test blueprint (细目表) assembly for practice tests.** Source: 菁优网/组卷网 specification-table assembly; patent CN109783601A greedy algorithm with required mastery level, difficulty window and no-repeat coverage [V]. Fit: an exam goal stores a blueprint (skills, weight, required level, item-type counts, time); the practice-test builder fills it greedily from the highest-required uncovered skill, with difficulty set by generator parameters. Cost: medium; this is the backbone of mock exams.

**4. Prerequisite back-tracing on repeated failure.** Source: iFlytek T90 (trace junior-high gaps to primary school), SMZDM (cross-grade prerequisite gaps) [V, low reliability]. Fit: after N lapses on a skill, or when "What went wrong?" says "didn't know the concept", run a short probe on its direct prerequisites in the skill graph and promote the weakest one; the same walk-down powers the placement test. Cost: medium; vendor evidence only, but natural on a graph.

**5. Hint ladder for step-level diagnosis (hint, not answer).** Source: 学而思 小思AI 1对1 (grades each step, hints instead of answers), iFlytek 晓悦 heuristic hints [V]; Rest of World reports unreliability of automated scanning [A]. Fit: "What went wrong?" escalates: step category (setup, algebra, arithmetic, misread), then a hint without the answer, then the exact step, then the full solution; hint depth is logged and feeds the grade. No handwriting recognition: typed steps, or "tap the first line where your work diverged" against the stored solution path of the generated problem. Cost: medium.

**6.
