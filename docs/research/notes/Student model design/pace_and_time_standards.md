# Course pace, workload and exam-timing standards from universities and exam boards

Prepared 2026-11-02 for Toishi. Facts and arithmetic only: nothing here recommends a standard or a design. Sources: official course, registrar, catalog and exam-board pages, and MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW). Web search ran out of quota partway; later sources came by direct fetch. Bot-blocked pages were not worked around.

Legend: **(d)** = my arithmetic on the cited numbers, not a statement by the source. **min/prob** = sitting minutes / numbered problems (a problem with parts counts once). **min/pt** = sitting minutes / total points. **L-Lab-P** = MIT unit triple (class hours, lab hours, preparation hours per week). **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed on a primary page. OCW base = `https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/<slug>/` (pages `/pages/syllabus/`, `/calendar/`, `/exams/`; exam PDFs under `/resources/`). OCW slugs: 18-01-single-variable-calculus-fall-2006; 18-02-multivariable-calculus-fall-2007; 18-03-differential-equations-spring-2010; 18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010; 8-01l-physics-i-classical-mechanics-fall-2005; 8-01sc-classical-mechanics-fall-2016; 8-02-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-spring-2007; 6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007; 6-003-signals-and-systems-fall-2011.

### 2.2 Topic spans per session (OCW calendars)

- **18.01 (F2006).** S1-7 derivative as slope/velocity, limits, product/quotient/trig rules, chain rule, implicit differentiation, exp/log; S8 Exam 1. S9-16 linear/quadratic approximation, curve sketching, max-min, related rates, Newton's method, MVT, antiderivatives, separable ODEs; S17 Exam 2. S18-24 definite integral, FTC 1 and 2, volumes, work/average value/probability, numerical integration; S25 review; S26 trig integrals; S27 Exam 3. S28-32 inverse substitution, partial fractions, parts, parametric/arclength, polar; S33 Exam 4. S34-37 L'Hopital, improper integrals, series/convergence tests, Taylor series; S38 review. Sessions with new content: 32 (d).
- **18.02 (F2007).** L0-7 vectors, dot/cross product, determinants, matrices, planes, parametric curves, velocity/acceleration; Exam 1. L8-15 partial derivatives, tangent planes, max-min/least squares, chain rule, gradient, Lagrange multipliers, PDEs; Exam 2. L16-24 double integrals, line integrals, path independence, potentials, Green's theorem, flux; Exam 3. L25-32 triple integrals, surface integrals/flux, divergence theorem, curl, Stokes; Exam 4. L33-35 Maxwell's equations, final review.
- **18.03 (S2010).** First-order (R1 to L9): direction fields, Euler's method, linear equations/integrating factors, complex exponentials, sinusoidal response, phase line/stability; Exam I. Second-order (L11 to L18): characteristic polynomial, damping, exponential response, complex gain, resonance, frequency response, RLC; Exam II (L19). Fourier and Laplace (L20 to L30): Fourier series, step/delta functions, impulse response, convolution, Laplace transform, poles, transfer function; Exam III. Systems (L32 to L39): eigenvalues/eigenvectors, phase plane, matrix exponential, nonlinear systems/linearization, limit cycles and chaos.
- **18.06 (S2010).** S1-11 elimination, LU, vector spaces, nullspace, echelon form, basis/dimension, four subspaces; S12 Exam 1 (chapters 1 to 3.4). S13-23 graphs, orthogonality, projections, least squares, Gram-Schmidt/QR, determinants (3 sessions), eigenvalues, diagonalization, Markov matrices; S24 review; S25 Exam 2. S26-36 differential equations, symmetric/positive-definite matrices, similar matrices, SVD, Fourier/FFT, linear transformations, change of basis, linear programming, review; S37 Exam 3. S38-39 numerical linear algebra, computational science; S40 final.
- **8.01L (F2005).** L1-4 vectors/statics/free-body diagrams; L5-7 kinematics (1D, 2D, circular); L8-12 Newton's laws, friction, springs; L13-16 work, energy, potential energy; L17-20 momentum, collisions, impulse, center of mass; L21-24 simple harmonic motion, gravity; L25 fluids; L26-33 rotation (torque, energy, angular momentum); L34 final review.
- **8.02 (S2007), by week.** 1 E-fields, charge distributions; 2 potential; 3 Gauss's law; 4 conductors/capacitors; 5 current, DC circuits; 6-7 magnetic fields (Biot-Savart, Ampere, forces on charges/dipoles/loops); 8-9 Faraday, inductors, RL; 10-11 RC/RL, LC, driven LRC; 12-13 Maxwell's equations, EM radiation; 14 interference, final.
- **6.002 (S2007).** L1-3 KVL/KCL, nodal analysis, Thevenin; L4-5 digital abstraction, MOS switch models; L6-7 nonlinear resistors, small signal; L8-9 dependent sources, MOS amplifier (Q1); L10-12 amplifier large/small signal; L13-15 first-order circuits, digital memory; L16-17 second-order systems; L18 sinusoidal steady state (Q2); L19-20 impedance, filters; L21-23 op-amps, feedback; L24-25 energy/power.
- **6.003 (F2011).** L1-4 signals, DT/CT systems, feedback and poles; L5-8 Z transform, Laplace, convolution (Exam 1 at L8); L9-13 frequency response, Bode plots, feedback and control (Exam 2 at L13); L14-19 Fourier series/transform, DT Fourier, relations (Exam 3 at L19); L20-25 applications, sampling, quantization, modulation, audio CD, final.
- **6.300 (F2026).** Fourier series, sampling/aliasing, DT Fourier series, CT/DT Fourier transform, systems, convolution, frequency response, DFT, STFT, FFT, 2D FT, DCT/JPEG.

### 2.4 MIT term regulations (catalog 2026-27, https://catalog.mit.edu/mit/procedures/term-regulations-exam-policies)

- Final exams are held 9:00 to 12:00 or 1:30 to 4:30, and "must be scheduled to last at least one hour and not more than three hours".
- Undergraduate tests outside class time "not exceed two hours", begin no earlier than 7:30 p.m. in the evening, and may not be held on Monday nights.
- Classes begin five minutes after and end five minutes before the scheduled hour or half-hour (a 1-h slot is 50 minutes).

## 5. Expert versus novice solving times
