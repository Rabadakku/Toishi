# Calculus I pacing at US universities, Chinese calculus calendars, and prior-knowledge studies

Prepared 2026-11-02 for Toishi. Facts and arithmetic only: nothing here recommends a design. Sources are course, department and instructor pages plus paper abstracts or full texts, fetched directly. Pages that failed, were bot-blocked or robots-blocked were not worked around (section 4).

Legend: **(d)** = my arithmetic or inference, not stated by the source. **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed on a primary page. Dates are M/D; **d** = calendar days (breaks included); **w** = instructional week. Resolution: **day** = every lecture dated; **week** = topics by week only; **exams only** = no topic calendar. A topic's offset runs from its first lecture to the first exam whose coverage includes it, or to the due date of the homework covering it. **†** = exam coverage not stated; I assumed it is everything taught before the exam. Each course's URLs are in 1.1; later tables cite the course name.
