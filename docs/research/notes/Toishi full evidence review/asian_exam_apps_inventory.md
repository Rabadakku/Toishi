## 1. What do the gaokao practice-book families (五年高考三年模拟, 天利38套, 高考必刷题, 金考卷, 一本涂书, 高考真题分类汇编) contain, and which mechanics do they imply?

### Takeaway
Publisher and retailer blurbs agree on a layered architecture: topic-centred (考点) training, then real papers regrouped by topic (真题分类), then timed mock papers (模拟卷/联考卷), with answers in a full-solution volume. The structural ideas worth borrowing are 必刷题's eight named training layers, 轻松刷's timed small-question sets (12 multiple-choice plus 4 fill-in), and topic-regrouped real papers. I could not reach table-of-contents-level detail, difficulty marks (★) or answer-analysis templates for 5·3, 天利38套, 金考卷 or 一本涂书.

### Inferences
- Across these books the same pipeline repeats: exam points (考点) first, then topic modules, then timed mixed sets, then recombined or regrouped real papers, with solutions in a separate analysis volume. Toishi already has an analogue of each stage: core band and tiers 1-5 ([spec ENG-4](/docs/spec.md)), Focused Practice by section (QUE-14), the timed-section test (QUE-18), full tests, and step-tagged worked solutions. The books are static; the differentiators ME adds are scheduling and diagnosis.
- 轻松刷's "12 multiple-choice plus 4 fill-in" timed set equals the first 16 items of the 12+4+6 layout in the mock papers (section 8). It maps onto QUE-18's "timed section" type without a new test type.
- 作业帮's four-way source label (textbook-traced / real / mock / original) is a student-visible provenance tag. ME tracks provenance in the content pipeline (including an "AI-original ... Gaokao- and CSAT-style" class), but I found no student-facing label. Since ME cannot ship real papers (the spec's Later item says redistribution has to be checked), an honest "original, gaokao-style" chip is the equivalent.
- Real-paper regrouping by topic (真题分类) is the inverse of a blueprint; it suggests nothing new for ME beyond section-scoped practice.

### Gaps
- Table-of-contents or column (栏目) lists of the modern 5·3, 天利38套, 金考卷, 一本涂书 and 十年高考真题分类汇编 volumes; whether 5·3 A and B editions differ in difficulty; any ★ difficulty markings; the answer-and-analysis template (for example whether it prints an easy-mistake warning) — Douban gave only short blurbs, JD, Dangdang and Tmall pages did not load, and Zhihu and Baidu Baike were blocked.
- No Douban hit for a 十年高考真题分类汇编 title.

## 2. How does 智学网's 个性化学习手册 (personalized learning handbook, "个册") work, and what do the school platforms 极课大数据 and 七天网络 (好分数/七天学堂) add?

### Takeaway
The handbook is a weekly, per-student printed loose-leaf built from the week's exam: the student's wrong questions, cause analysis, correction space and about three same-type items on the weak knowledge point. Outcome claims are vendor-reported without controls. The two technical difficulties iFlytek names (which knowledge point caused an error when a question covers several; keeping the similar questions tied to the original weak point) are exactly what step-level tapping in ME is built to solve.

### Inferences
- The handbook loop is: scan the exam, tag errors to knowledge points, push about three same-type items, print, student corrects on the page, next week's exam closes the loop. ME's counterparts are STA-14 (per-miss record with cause and correction), QUE-20 (fresh problems on open mistakes) and the Later item "PDF retest papers". Only the printed delivery is missing.
- iFlytek's first named difficulty (multi-knowledge-point attribution) is addressed in ME by the tapped step: the tap puts 0.6 of the blame on that step's skill (0.8 if it matched a known wrong answer) and ENG-15 spreads blame otherwise. The second (keeping variants tied to the weak point) follows if QUE-20 selects on the tapped step's skill rather than the problem's main skill.
- Outcome numbers (76.4% to 82.4%, "7.5倍", 91% renewal) are class-level, uncontrolled and vendor-reported; they show adoption, not learning gain. The "at least half an hour" quote [A] is the cost the handbook removes, and automatic capture of misses in ME removes the same cost.
- I read no store reviews, so I do not know why school-platform ratings are so low.

### Gaps
- No independent evaluation of the handbook; no image of a handbook page; how many items per weak point beyond "three" or "2-3"; whether the weekly cadence (exam, handbook, retest) is fixed or configurable.
- 智学网 student-app feature set beyond the description (release notes are boilerplate); 智学网's own site (zhixue.com) was blocked.
- 极课大数据 current product pages (no iOS app found; the PitchHub entry gives corporate facts only); 七天网络's school product documents; 好分数 student mistake-book features.

## 3. What do the Chinese consumer apps and devices (洋葱学园, 快对, 一起作业, 小盒科技, 题拍拍, 夸克, 有道, 高途) offer for practice?

### Takeaway
Consumer apps compete on photo search plus AI explanation (快对, 小猿搜题, 夸克) and on adaptive video courses (洋葱, 高途). ## 8. What does a Chinese student need that Toishi does not yet have: interface, notation, input, fonts and gaokao marking?

### Takeaway
Beyond translating the interface, the work is: CJK fonts and language tagging (the app is offline and Mac-only), input handling (IME composition in MathLive on WKWebView, full-width punctuation), Chinese notation (answers print intervals with "+∞", angle brackets mean the angle between vectors, lg for base 10) and a rubric-based, cumulative step-mark model for 解答题. Much of the Chinese-specific notation is not sourced in what I reached; the unsourced items are in Gaps, not asserted.

### Cited Findings
#### Interface language and glyph variants
- The spec lists "Chinese interface and content for gaokao users" as a Later item: "A Chinese interface and packs for gaokao candidates. It needs translation and a check of which past papers may be redistributed" — [spec Later](/docs/spec.md).
- One code point (for example 草, U+8349) is shared across Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and "The browser should select, for each character, a glyph (from a font) suitable to the specified language"; CJK fonts need language-specific variants — [Wikipedia, Han unification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_unification) [A].
- Adobe Source Han Sans: SIL Open Font License 1.1; "Changing languages is accomplished by either selecting the font of the desired language or by language-tagging the text"; region-specific subsets for CN, TW, HK, JP and KR, and WOFF2 variable fonts; the README gives no file sizes — [Source Han Sans README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans/release/README.md) [V].

#### Fonts and rendering in this app
- The spec sets problem text in Source Serif 4 and the interface in Inter, both bundled; the page is served from a loopback server with a strict content security policy ("its own origin only; no remote scripts, fonts or connections", ARC-2) — [spec design and ARC-2](/docs/spec.md). KaTeX 0.19.1 is bundled and content builds render in strict mode (MATH-1).
- Apple's system-font list excerpt shows Heiti SC (Light, Medium) as macOS system fonts, while Kaiti SC (Black, Bold, Regular) and Hei Regular are "downloadable"; the excerpt was cut off before PingFang SC and Songti SC — [Apple system fonts](https://developer.apple.com/fonts/system-fonts/) [V].
- KaTeX: CJK glyphs are always accepted in `\text` mode; they render from system fonts, so typography may clash; the CSS classes `.cjk_fallback` and relatives set fallback fonts; alignment is approximate because other glyphs are treated as being as tall as "M" — [KaTeX supported functions](https://katex.org/docs/supported) [V]. How strict mode treats CJK outside `\text` was not confirmed (the page excerpt was cut off).

#### Typed input
- MathLive on IME composition [A, bug reports]: issue 3058 (open, 2026-08): `onCompositionStart` deletes the selection unconditionally (Microsoft Bopomofo, v0.110.0, Chrome on Windows); the proposed fix skips the delete for a collapsed selection and a workaround is a capture-phase stopImmediatePropagation listener — [issue 3058](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/3058). Issue 2465 (open, 2024): typed CJK characters are not visible when the expression gets long (v0.101.0, macOS 14.4.1, Chrome) — [issue 2465](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/2465). Issue 555 (closed, 2020): CJK ignored until another key is typed; follow-ups report characters disappearing on macOS Safari and compositions failing to restart or duplicating in other browsers — [issue 555](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/555). Titles seen but not read: [1071](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/1071) (Simplified Chinese virtual keyboard), [2373](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/2373) (Chinese locale config invalid), [1556](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/1556) (East-Asian cursor order in fractions), [1734](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/1734) (iPad composition), [2934](https://github.com/arnog/mathlive/issues/2934) (translation error).
- The app's shell is pywebview over macOS's WKWebView and MathLive 0.111 or later (0.111 fixes WebKit losing the last edit when a card advances) — [spec architecture](/docs/spec.md). WKWebView is Safari's engine, so the Safari-specific reports (issue 555 follow-ups) are the relevant ones.
- Mainland Chinese punctuation follows GB/T 15834-2011 and uses full-width marks (。，、：；！？); for technical books with many formulas the full stop may be unified with the Western period — [W3C clreq](https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/) [V].
- Unicode U+FF01-FF5E reproduces ASCII 21-7E as fullwidth forms, with the fullwidth comma at U+FF0C — [Wikipedia, Halfwidth and Fullwidth Forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halfwidth_and_Fullwidth_Forms_%28Unicode_block%29) [A]. Unicode Normalization Form KC "folds the differences between compatibility-equivalent characters", "must not be blindly applied to arbitrary text", and some compatibility characters "are used in mathematical notation to represent a distinction of a semantic nature" — [UAX #15](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/) [V].
- Local check [E, my own run of Python's unicodedata, NFKC]: fullwidth "（１，２）" becomes "(1,2)", "｛ｘ｜ｘ＞２｝" becomes "{x|x>2}", "，；：" become ",;:", but "。" and "、" are unchanged; NFKC also flattens "x²" to "x2", "C₅²" to "C52" and "…" to "...". No URL: reproducible with `unicodedata.normalize('NFKC', s)`.
- The spec's answer syntax [spec-defined]: `ln(x)` natural log, `log(x)` base 10, `log_2(x)` base 2; constants `pi`, `e`, `inf`; `interval` such as `(-inf, 0) U (2, inf)` (inequalities also accepted, `endpoints: either` optional); `set` as an enumerated list (`-1, 0, 1`, `none` for empty); `|x|` or `abs(x)`; vector `<1, -2, 3>` and point `(2, -4)`; lists separated by `;`; number answers accept decimals within 0.1% unless `exact: yes`. A grep of [card-format.md](/docs/card-format.md) and the spec found no mention of `lg`, `+inf`, Unicode symbols (∞, ∪, ≤, ≥, −) or fullwidth normalization [E, local grep].

#### Notation conventions
- zh.wikipedia lists ISO 31-11 notation: lg = log base 10, ln = natural log, lb = log base 2 — [zh.wikipedia 对数](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%AF%B9%E6%95%B0) [A]. In the text of both mock answer keys below, "ln" occurs and "lg" and "log" do not [E, local grep of extracted text].
- Natural numbers (自然数): mainland textbooks before about 2000 excluded 0 and most after about 2000 include it; GB 3102.11-93 (ISO 31-11-based) defines "非负整数集;自然数集" and has been recommended-only since 2017-03-23 — [zh.wikipedia 自然数](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%87%AA%E7%84%B6%E6%95%B0) [A]. The N* and N+ symbols were stripped from my extraction, so they are unverified.
- English Wikipedia lists "tan or tg" and "cot or ctg" as abbreviations, with nothing China-specific — [Wikipedia, Trigonometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometric_functions) [A].
- How answers are printed in two mock-exam answer keys [A, read as rendered pages; non-official school mocks; undated; both use the 12+4+6 layout]: fill-in intervals "(√e, +∞)" (item 15 of the 江苏淮安 key) and "(3/2, +∞)" (item 16 of the 山东泰安 key); a domain stated as "函数 f(x) 的定义域是 (0, +∞)"; a parameter range stated as "实数 a 的取值范围是 (e/2, e²/4]"; monotonic intervals such as "在(−∞, x1)上单调递增"; other fill-in answers "0.9", "2 − √3", "√13/13", "4√21π/3" and "5√13/52" (the cosine of a dihedral angle, "夹角余弦值为"); a degree written "∠CAD = 90⁰"; vectors printed bold italic with "cos⟨m, n⟩" denoting the angle between vectors; sub-question labels in full-width parentheses "（1）" "（2）"; each 解答题 header carries its points, "17.（10 分）"; solutions open with "解：" and use "证明：", "因为…所以…", "令", "则", "又", "即", "综上可得，当 m≤0 时…", "只需证…" — [泰安 key](https://mjy.js.org/resources/%E8%BF%91%E6%9C%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E5%90%84%E5%9C%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E6%A8%A1%E6%8B%9F%E5%8D%B7/%E3%80%90%E6%96%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E3%80%91%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%9C%E6%B3%B0%E5%AE%89%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%899%E6%9C%88%E9%98%B6%E6%AE%B5%E6%B5%8B%E8%AF%95%EF%BC%889.30%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%8C%E9%AB%98%E6%B8%85%EF%BC%89/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%AD%94%E6%A1%88.pdf), [淮安 key](https://mjy.js.org/resources/%E8%BF%91%E6%9C%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E5%90%84%E5%9C%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E6%A8%A1%E6%8B%9F%E5%8D%B7/%E3%80%90%E6%96%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E3%80%91%E6%B1%9F%E8%8B%8F%E6%B7%AE%E5%AE%89%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%89%E5%BC%80%E5%AD%A6%E8%80%83%EF%BC%889.26%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%89/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%AD%94%E6%A1%88.pdf).
- zh.wikipedia writes set-builder notation as `{x ∈ R | x² > 1}` with a vertical bar (Wikipedia's own notation, not proof of textbook practice) — [zh.wikipedia 集合](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%9B%86%E5%90%88_%28%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%29) [A].

#### Gaokao paper structure and marking
- Structure per zh.wikipedia (page c. 2021) [A]: 新课标卷 math is 150 points in 120 minutes (12 multiple-choice x 5, 4 fill-in x 5, 5 required 解答题 x 12, plus one elective of 10); 新高考: items 1-8 single-choice, 9-12 multi-select, 13-16 fill-in (one may have two blanks), item 17 worth 10 and items 18-22 worth 12 each, no elective — [zh.wikipedia 高考](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%99%AE%E9%80%9A%E9%AB%98%E7%AD%89%E5%AD%A6%E6%A0%A1%E6%8B%9B%E7%94%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E7%BB%9F%E4%B8%80%E8%80%83%E8%AF%95). The mock keys' headers match the 新高考 layout: "二、选择题：本题共 4 小题，每小题 5 分，共 20 分" (multi-select) and "四、解答题：本题共 6 小题，共 70 分" (70 of 150 points) — [泰安 key](https://mjy.js.org/resources/%E8%BF%91%E6%9C%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E5%90%84%E5%9C%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E6%A8%A1%E6%8B%9F%E5%8D%B7/%E3%80%90%E6%96%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E3%80%91%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%9C%E6%B3%B0%E5%AE%89%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%899%E6%9C%88%E9%98%B6%E6%AE%B5%E6%B5%8B%E8%AF%95%EF%BC%889.30%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%8C%E9%AB%98%E6%B8%85%EF%BC%89/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%AD%94%E6%A1%88.pdf) [A].
- Multi-select partial credit in a mock key: "全部选对的得 5 分，部分选对的得 2 分，有选错的得 0 分" (all correct 5, partly correct 2, any wrong 0) — [泰安 key](https://mjy.js.org/resources/%E8%BF%91%E6%9C%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E5%90%84%E5%9C%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E6%A8%A1%E6%8B%9F%E5%8D%B7/%E3%80%90%E6%96%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E3%80%91%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%9C%E6%B3%B0%E5%AE%89%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%899%E6%9C%88%E9%98%B6%E6%AE%B5%E6%B5%8B%E8%AF%95%EF%BC%889.30%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%8C%E9%AB%98%E6%B8%85%EF%BC%89/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%AD%94%E6%A1%88.pdf) [A].
- Step marks in the keys are cumulative: right-aligned "2 分", "4 分", ... "12 分" after blocks of steps in the 淮安 key, and a dotted leader with "………1 分", "……2 分", "……4 分", "……5 分", "……6 分" in the 泰安 key; alternative methods appear as "解法二：" or "或：" with marks on the same scale, and notes such as "注：第二问用等积法、综合法等方法解答同样给分" (other methods for part 2, such as equal-volume or synthetic methods, earn the same marks) — [淮安 key](https://mjy.js.org/resources/%E8%BF%91%E6%9C%9F%E5%85%A8%E5%9B%BD%E5%90%84%E5%9C%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E6%A8%A1%E6%8B%9F%E5%8D%B7/%E3%80%90%E6%96%B0%E9%AB%98%E8%80%83%E3%80%91%E6%B1%9F%E8%8B%8F%E6%B7%AE%E5%AE%89%E9%AB%98%E4%B8%89%E5%BC%80%E5%AD%A6%E8%80%83%EF%BC%889.26%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%89/%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%AD%94%E6%A1%88.pdf) [A].
- Sina (2026-06-06) [A]: marking is step-based, "整个判卷流程完全按步骤给分" (the whole marking process gives marks strictly by steps), step marks carry a high weight ("对步骤分的权重设置较高"), and a blank answer scores zero ("空白即为零分"); the tip to write "解" first comes from a CCTV short video quoting a TV drama and is not an official rule — [Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/discovery/2026-06-06/doc-iniamhua7894266.shtml).
- Toutiao article [A]: a fill-in blank scores only if everything in it is correct ("只有全部写对才给分"); a blank needing two answers such as "18/5或0" earns zero if only one is written — [Toutiao](https://www.toutiao.com/zixun/7514094742138243087/).
- Spec hooks: MATH-16 (calculator allowed or not, default no; radians unless the card says degrees); the self-graded rubric (study screen); the "rubric" blueprint item kind (Later); the `set` answer type (same elements in any order) — [spec](/docs/spec.md), [card-format](/docs/card-format.md).

### Inferences
Localization requirements, each tied to its evidence (R = requirement):
- R1 Interface. Externalize all UI strings (the app is English-only) and add a zh-Hans locale; translate MathLive's own strings (virtual keyboard, menus); locale bugs exist upstream (titles of issues 2373 and 2934, not read). Rename parser error messages as part of this, since the card format's syntax errors are shown to the student.
- R2 Language tagging. Mark Chinese text with `lang="zh-Hans"` so WebKit picks Simplified-Chinese glyph forms (Han unification; Source Han README); mark mixed content at element level.
- R3 CJK font. The app is offline with a strict content security policy (no remote fonts), so the CJK font must be local: either system fonts (Heiti SC is a system font per Apple's excerpt; PingFang SC and Songti SC availability is unverified) or a bundled, subsetted Source Han Sans/Serif SC under the OFL. Source Serif 4 and Inter are Latin-script families, and I believe they have no CJK glyphs (not verified), so a CJK fallback stack is needed for both UI and problem text, plus `.cjk_fallback` for CJK inside KaTeX `\text{}`.
- R4 Strict builds. Keep CJK only inside `\text{}` in LaTeX so the strict-mode build (MATH-1) does not fail; test this in the build.
- R5 Input normalization. Add a targeted mapping of U+FF01-FF5E (full-width ASCII range) and a few CJK marks to their ASCII forms in the plain-text answer parser, because a Chinese IME in Chinese mode emits "，", "（", "）", "；", "：". Do not apply blanket NFKC: my local run shows it flattens superscripts and subscripts and ellipses, which UAX #15 warns about for mathematical notation.
- R6 Symbols in typed answers. The keys print "+∞", "−∞", "∞", "−" and "√"; the card syntax lists `inf` and ASCII operators only. Accept `+inf` and the Unicode forms (∞, ∪, U+2212 minus, ≤, ≥, ×, ·) if the parser does not already.
- R7 Logarithms. Accept `lg(x)` as base 10 and `lb(x)` as base 2 (ISO 31-11 per zh.wikipedia), keeping `log(x)` base 10 per the spec; check that a variable base such as log_a(x) parses. Textbook usage of lg versus log in China is not sourced (Gaps).
- R8 Interval and set answers. Chinese keys print "+∞" and use parentheses and brackets for intervals, so interval answers will be typed that way. Whether domain and range answers must be a set or interval (not an inequality) is not sourced; if it holds, a stricter checker option (inequalities rejected) would be needed because the spec accepts `x < 0 or x > 2` for intervals.
- R9 Set-builder and bars. If set-builder `{x | x > 2}` is accepted in plain text, its bar collides with `|x|` for absolute value; the parser needs a disambiguation rule (inference from the card syntax and the Wikipedia example).
- R10 Vector notation. The keys use ⟨m, n⟩ for the angle between vectors, while the card format's vector answer is `<1, -2, 3>`; a Chinese-locale card should display component vectors in parentheses (the textbook convention for components is unsourced), and the checker should accept both.
- R11 Exact answers. Gaokao keys print exact forms (√13/13, 4√21π/3) and sometimes decimals (0.9); the default `number` tolerance (0.1% for decimals) is looser than a fill-in key, so gaokao-style cards should set `exact: yes` or a `form` option (inference).
- R12 Marking model. Represent each 解答题 solution as marked steps with cumulative marks, optional alternate methods ("解法二") and a note field ("注"); self-marking ticks mark segments and totals the score (the "rubric" kind, currently Later). Support item kinds for single-choice, multi-select with partial credit (5/2/0 in the mocks; the official rule is unverified), fill-in with several blanks (the `set` answer type gives all-or-nothing) and 解答题 with variable marks. Item labels use full-width parentheses "（1）" and the "N 分" suffix.
- R13 IME testing. Test pinyin and zhuyin composition in the packaged WKWebView with MathLive: MathLive's composition bugs are open or were fixed only in some browsers. If composition fails, route Chinese-character answers to a native text input rather than the math field.
- R14 Calculator default. MATH-16's default of no calculator suits gaokao-style cards; I did not verify the gaokao calculator policy (Gaps).
