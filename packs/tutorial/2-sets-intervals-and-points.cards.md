## Card tutorial.intervals.001
skill: tutorial.intervals
answer-type: interval
answer: [1, 3)
vars: x
wrong: (1, 3) => The square bracket means 1 is included: start with [.

### Question
Type the interval \([1, 3)\).

### Hints
1. `[` and `]` include an endpoint; `(` and `)` leave it out.

### Solution
0. [tutorial.intervals] Type it: `[1, 3)`.

## Card tutorial.sets.001

### Hints
1. Separate the elements with commas. The braces are optional, and the order doesn't matter.

0. [tutorial.sets] Type it: `-1, 2` (or `{-1, 2}`, or `2, -1`).
